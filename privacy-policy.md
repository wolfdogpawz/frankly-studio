# Privacy Policy for FlowStateADHD

**Last updated: 3 June 2026 (subscriptions added)**
**Developer: Frankly Studio**

## Overview

FlowStateADHD is a reading tool built for people with ADHD, dyslexia, and reading difficulties. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

## Data we collect

FlowStateADHD does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal content. All text you paste or type into the app stays entirely on your device. Your reading history, settings, style presets, and saved Kindle email address are stored locally using your device's own storage and are never sent to us or any server we operate.

The only exception is subscription purchases. If you subscribe to FlowStateADHD Pro, your purchase is processed by Google Play and by RevenueCat, our subscription-management provider (see "Subscriptions and payments" below). We do not see or store your payment-card details.

## Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

- **Camera** — used only to scan physical text for reading. Photos are processed on your device and never uploaded or stored.
- **Storage** — used to open Word (.docx) and plain text files you choose from your device.

No permission is used for any purpose beyond what is described above.

## Send to Kindle

When you use the Send to Kindle feature:

- Your text is written to a local HTML file on your device and attached to an email.
- The email is composed using your phone's own email app — it is sent from your account, not ours.
- The destination is the Kindle email address you entered in Settings; we never see this address.
- Once you send the email, Amazon receives the document directly. FlowStateADHD is not involved in delivery.

## Export as PDF

When you export a reading as PDF, the file is generated locally on your device and opened in your system share sheet so you can save or share it where you want. No copy of the PDF is sent to us.

## Text-to-speech

Text-to-speech uses your device's built-in speech engine. The text you read aloud is processed locally by your operating system. We do not record or transmit any audio.

## Subscriptions and payments

FlowStateADHD Pro is an optional paid subscription. When you subscribe:

- The purchase itself is handled entirely by **Google Play Billing**. We never see or store your card or payment details.
- We use **RevenueCat** (revenuecat.com) to manage and verify subscriptions and to unlock Pro features on your device. To do this, RevenueCat processes a limited amount of data: your purchase and subscription history, an anonymous app-user identifier, a device identifier, and your IP address (used to infer country for pricing and tax). This data is tied to an anonymous ID, not to your name or email.
- RevenueCat acts as our data processor under their privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- This data is used only to deliver and restore your subscription. It is never sold or used for advertising.

## Third party services

The app uses Expo (expo.dev) as its development framework. The OpenDyslexic, Rubik, and Atkinson Hyperlegible fonts are loaded from a public CDN (jsdelivr) the first time you open the app. Subscription management uses RevenueCat as described above. No analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools are included.

## Children

This app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
