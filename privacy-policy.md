# Privacy Policy for FlowStateADHD

**Last updated: 12 May 2025**
**Developer: Frankly Studio**

## Overview

FlowStateADHD is a reading tool built for people with ADHD and reading difficulties. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

## Data we collect

FlowStateADHD does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party.

All text you paste or type into the app stays entirely on your device. Your reading history, settings, and preferences are stored locally using your device's storage and are never sent anywhere.

## Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

- **Camera** — used only to scan physical text for reading. Photos are processed on your device and never uploaded or stored.
- **Storage** — used to open PDF and Word documents from your device.
- **Microphone** — used only for text-to-speech functionality.

No permission is used for any purpose beyond what is described above.

## Third party services

The app uses Expo (expo.dev) as its development framework. No analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools are included.

## Children

This app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
